# Serprove Vet

## The Product
Serprove Vet is an e-commerce and catalog platform tailored for veterinary supplies. It serves as a direct bridge between the supplier and veterinary clinics/professionals. 

## Core Workflows
1. **Catalog Browsing:** Users (veterinary professionals) can browse products, filter by category, see current stock, and identify special offers.
2. **Purchasing via WhatsApp:** Instead of a complex checkout funnel, users build a cart and finalize their order via a direct WhatsApp connection, fostering a personalized B2B relationship.
3. **Administration:** The platform includes a protected admin dashboard where the owner can manage inventory (CRUD operations on products and categories) and dynamically update site settings (colors, contact info, banners).

## Audience
Veterinary clinics, pet shops, and professionals who need a reliable, fast, and trustworthy interface to restock their supplies.

## The Vibe
Professional, clean, and direct. The platform must inspire trust and efficiency, avoiding unnecessary friction in the purchasing process.
